THE EMIKA PROCESS

From vision to reality.

A clear process from start to finish. We guide every build through six defined stages to keep timelines orderly and expectations aligned.

Stage 01 • Consultation
Stage 02 • Planning
Stage 03 • Pre-Construction
Stage 04 • Construction
Stage 05 • Quality Review
Stage 06 • Project Delivery
Active Stage Focus

01 • Consultation

Initial review of project goals, lifestyle requirements, site parameters, and preliminary budget considerations.

Step 01

Consultation

We meet to understand your goals, discuss site parameters, review initial priorities, and outline preliminary budget ranges.

Step 02

Planning

Architectural schematic development, material specifications, engineering consultations, and scheduled work plans.

Step 03

Pre-Construction

Permit preparation, trade team scheduling, procurement of primary structural materials, and site setup.

Step 04

Construction

Site framing, envelope sealing, mechanical rough-ins, and fine millwork with structured weekly progress reviews.

Step 05

Quality Review

Thorough quality inspection, detail touch-ups, systems testing, and finishing verification across all living areas.

Step 06

Project Delivery

Final principal-led walkthrough, key handover, project documentation, and maintenance recommendations.
