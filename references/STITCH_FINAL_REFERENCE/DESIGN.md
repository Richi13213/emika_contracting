## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an uncompromising visual language for luxury architectural construction, custom fabrication, and structural engineering. Built upon the lineage of multi-generational Canadian craftsmanship, the system synthesizes razor-sharp structural brutalism with the restrained elegance of contemporary European editorial publications.

The identity is anchored by three defining traits:
- **Isometric & Razor-Sharp Precision:** Direct visual harmony with the isometric monogram. Every line, boundary, and interface container is governed by strict 90-degree and 45-degree geometric logic.
- **Architectural Authority:** Deep structural contrast replaces transient consumer polish. Generous negative space, uncompromising black-line disciplines, and bold architectural blocks reflect monolithic mass, raw poured concrete, dark structural steel, and precision-milled timber.
- **Target Audience & Tone:** Designed for institutional clients, private estate developers, bespoke residential patrons, and world-class architectural collaborators. The emotional imprint is heavy, permanent, trustworthy, and rigorously disciplined.

## Colors

The chromatic strategy relies on tension between warm foundational matter, dark structural weight, and an electric technical signature:

- **Warm Off-White (`#F8F7F3`):** The primary canvas. Evoking hand-troweled limestone plaster, bleached oak, and refined drafting vellum, it softens digital harshness while providing an elevated baseline.
- **Primary Royal Blue (`#123DA6`):** The defining signature accent derived directly from architectural blueline drawings and the brand monogram. Used deliberately for focal commitment points, primary interactive states, key callouts, and structural stamps.
- **Charcoal (`#1A1A1A`):** The monolithic structural anchor. Deployed for typographic dominance, full-bleed engineered hero panels, technical specifications, and structural hairline partitions.
- **Cool Gray (`#8A9AA6`):** An anodized-aluminum midtone that balances the warmth of the background with the intense saturation of Royal Blue. Employed for secondary drafting annotations, dimension lines, phase indicators, and subdued interactive borders.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs architectural weight with technical precision:

- **Manrope (Display & Body):** Serves as the primary voice across narrative text and editorial headlines. Its geometric construction mirrors architectural modernism while maintaining maximum legibility at large scales when tightly tracked.
- **Space Grotesk (Drafting & Metadata):** A technical monospace-inflected sans applied exclusively to metadata, engineering data tables, phase designations, GPS coordinates, square footages, and interactive button triggers.
- **Stylistic Rules:**
  - Headlines use high-weight, negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.04em`) to communicate physical mass.
  - Labels and technical callouts are rendered strictly in uppercase with wide letter-spacing (`0.1em` to `0.14em`), directly referencing blueprint legends and fabrication schematics.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy is defined by a rigid asymmetric structural grid inspired by architectural blueprints:

- **Grid System:** 12-column fluid grid for desktop with 1.5rem gutters, reducing to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile.
- **Asymmetric Composition:** Content layouts resist standard symmetry. High-resolution imagery spans 7 or 8 columns, while offset technical data panels occupy the remaining 4 or 5 columns.
- **Hairline Blueprint Grids:** Major structural regions are demarcated by explicit 1px dividing lines rather than whitespace alone, creating the feel of a precision drafting sheet.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - Desktop: 1200px and above. Margins are 4rem; full architectural spreads take priority.
  - Tablet: 768px – 1199px. Margins collapse to 2rem; technical panels drop below visual plates.
  - Mobile: Under 768px. Margins set to 1.25rem; cards stack vertically with persistent single-pixel top and bottom dividers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects decorative drop shadows, glass reflections, and ambient blurs. Instead, spatial depth is established through tectonic planar layering:

- **Tonal Contrast Plazas:** Depth is conveyed by abutting flat slabs of solid color. Warm Off-White (`#F8F7F3`) panels sit directly beside or atop dense Charcoal (`#1A1A1A`) slabs or high-impact Royal Blue (`#123DA6`) blocks.
- **Low-Contrast Structural Outlines:** Surfaces are separated by 1px crisp borders. On light surfaces, divisions are drawn in `rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.12)`; on dark surfaces, hairlines use `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.14)`.
- **Z-Index Layering (Planar Drift):** Sticky editorial indices and navigation headers sit firmly on `z-index: 10` to `z-index: 50` with solid opaque backgrounds and clean bottom borders, allowing photographic spreads to pass beneath like raw materials moving across a cutting table.
- **Modal Elevation:** If an overlay or architectural drawer is triggered, ambient shadows are minimized to a tight directional drop: `0 16px 32px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.16)` without soft colored diffusion.

## Shapes

The shape language is strictly **sharp (0px border radius)** across all UI elements.

- **Crisp Structural Alignment:** Corners remain completely unrounded across buttons, inputs, imagery, badges, navigation bars, and cards. This reinforces the physical nature of structural steel, sawn lumber, cut stone, and the geometric facets of the monogram.
- **Geometric Monoliths:** Interactive elements present as clean rectangular blocks, utilizing exact 1px internal or bounding strokes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid Royal Blue (`#123DA6`) background, Warm Off-White (`#F8F7F3`) text, 0px border radius. Padding: `14px 28px`. Typography: `Space Grotesk` uppercase (`label-md`). Hover state transitions abruptly to Charcoal (`#1A1A1A`).
- **Secondary / Technical:** Transparent background, 1px solid Charcoal (`#1A1A1A`), Charcoal text. Hover state fills background with Royal Blue (`#123DA6`) and turns text to Warm Off-White.
- **Ghost Action:** Text-only with an isometric directional arrow (`↗` or `→`). Underline animates outward from center on hover.

### Form Inputs & Fields
- **Text Fields:** Minimalist drafting style with no side or top borders; single 1px solid bottom border (`rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.2)`). Transparent background. Active/focused state turns bottom border to Royal Blue (`#123DA6`) at 2px thickness with an uppercase `Space Grotesk` floating label in Cool Gray (`#8A9AA6`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Unrounded 16x16px square containers with a 1px solid Charcoal border. Checked state fills solid with Royal Blue (`#123DA6`) with an internal geometric marker.

### Cards & Project Showcases
- **Architectural Project Cards:** Edge-to-edge photography bound by 1px solid lines. Hover initiates a slow, measured scale (`scale(1.02)`) over `600ms cubic-bezier(0.25, 1, 0.5, 1)` contained within an `overflow: hidden` frame.
- **Metadata Legend:** Below each card, a technical strip displays phase code, square footage, and structural material specs set in `Space Grotesk` (`label-md`).

### Chips & Status Badges
- **Specification Chips:** Zero-radius rectangular markers. 1px solid Cool Gray (`#8A9AA6`) border, transparent background, text in Charcoal (`#1A1A1A`) at `label-sm`.
- **Status Badges:** Pinned badges on project imagery feature solid Royal Blue (`#123DA6`) or solid Charcoal (`#1A1A1A`) fills with high-contrast text.

### Architectural Blueprint Grid (Specialized Component)
- **Technical Drawing Wrapper:** Interactive CAD/drawing viewer surrounded by a 1px technical border with coordinate ticks (`A1`, `B2`, etc.) in Cool Gray on the perimeter. Panning tools use sharp square buttons docked to the bottom right.

### Project Metric Counter
- **Display Block:** Monolithic Charcoal block with large Manrope numerals (`display-xl`) in Warm Off-White, accompanied by a Cool Gray sub-label in `Space Grotesk` describing square footage, structural load, or project value.
